Because the Internet: Childish Gambino's Cinematic Album

Troy from Community is one very talented man. Depending on which version of him you met first — Childish Gambino, Donald Glover, or Earn from Atlanta — you already know he does everything, and does it with excellence.

Music & Film • 5 min read • Because the Internet, 2013

For us, it goes back to his Community days in 2009. It's safe to say Donald Glover has exceeded whatever expectations we had of him since.

Because the Internet is cinematic because it's much more than 57 minutes spread across 19 songs. Released in 2013 as Childish Gambino's second studio album, it was recorded at a mansion he was renting at the time from former NBA player Chris Bosh — which tells you something about the scale of ambition here before you've even hit play.

The Credits

  • Released: 2013 — Childish Gambino's second studio album
  • Recorded at: A mansion rented from former NBA player Chris Bosh
  • Featured artists: Chance the Rapper, Problem, Jhené Aiko, Kilo Kish, Azealia Banks
  • Production: Ludwig Göransson (Atlanta, Community, Black Panther, The Odyssey) and Gambino himself, among others

Speaking of cinematic — that ensemble cast and Göransson's fingerprints are exactly why the word fits. This isn't a guest-verse album; it's a produced one, in the fullest sense.

Why Atlanta happened here

Glover has talked about wanting to create, write, act in, and produce his own show — the ambition that became Atlanta. Pursuing it meant scaling back his role on Community, and that gap is exactly where this album was made. Less TV, more studio time, and a mansion to do it in.

What "Because the Internet" actually means

“Because the internet I'm here, because the internet we're all here. It's the language of earth. Everyone keeps saying by this or that year, Mandarin or Spanish will be the most dominant language, but the internet is already a language we are all connected to; even my dad can understand the meme format. But the thing is, there are no rules, which is also the awesome thing.”

— Childish Gambino

Internet ubuntu, we'd like to believe.

The screenplay nobody finished

Because the Internet came with its own screenplay, built around a central character called The Boy — living in a mansion, spending his days tweeting at celebrities and posting videos. Had it been fully developed, the film would have brought in Chance the Rapper, Rick Ross, and Jhené Aiko as supporting characters. It never got made, but the screenplay is still out there: read it on archive.org.
