Why festivals and concerts are better than listening to music alone.

Music is well established to change or adapt to the environment or medium in which it is heard. A clear example is the difference between live and recorded music. Recorded music can be too polished, while live music carries an aura of unpredictability. Live music is communal. It has themes, settings, and mini experiences that keep things fun without ever getting boring.

Here are some reasons why we prefer live music over recorded music:

Why Live Wins

  1. Festivals deliver on shared musical experiences. While listening to music at home is great, festivals bring together like-minded people outside your circle of friends. Quite often, the strangers you engage with at festivals tend to become friends through shared experience. It is also validating to see others enjoy that which you enjoy.
  2. One of the best things about festivals is the direct engagement that artists have with fans. The digital barrier is non-existent, allowing for mosh pits, stage call-ups and even remixes and repeats of classics. There exists a shared identity where the artist gets to "collaborate" with the people that they make music for: the people that make the artists wealthy and relevant, thanks to the fans' money spent on tickets, streams and merchandise.
  3. Without a doubt, you leave festivals with new musicians to check out. Festivals are stacked with artists. Being exposed to a variety of artists is bound to introduce you to music that you either weren't aware of or didn't care about. This tends to leave you a fan of the classics, while also being introduced to the latest work of the artists. This evolves your musical palette as you begin to merge your traditional music and the contemporary music of today.
  4. Due to a combination of high prices and scarcity of festivals, festivals tend to become more memorable. Streaming has made recorded music so voluminous that people no longer form a special bond with music appreciation. Recorded music is devalued in terms of money and impact. Artists now make the majority of their income from festivals and tours.
  5. Festival fever is above the small concerts you have in your head when alone in your room. Picture multiple stages, food and make-up stalls and walking space while everyone is dressed to the 10's, listening to some of your favourite artists serenade you as the sky transitions from an orange hue to a black sky illuminated by the neon lights found all over the festival grounds.
  6. It is not every day that you get to see your favourite artist live. For this reason, it becomes a very emotional space where you get to vibe with the author of that song or album on your phone. You can become overcome with emotion if you recall what that song has gotten you through: that break-up, that road trip or even that video game you grew up playing as a child. This is what you'll never forget.

There are a lot of events this summer. Make sure to go out, meet new people and have new experiences.
