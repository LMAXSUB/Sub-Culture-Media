Who We Are

Sub Culture is a media house covering culture, music, style and the stories behind them — album reviews, personal essays, cover features, and lifestyle pieces, backed by our own photography and videography. We're not a review site that reposts press releases; every feature is written properly, and every photo is shot for it.

How to Pitch Us

  1. Reach out by WhatsApp or email with a short pitch — who you are, what you'd like covered, and why it fits Sub Culture.

  2. Include any existing material that helps us understand the story — music links, photos, a website, social handles.

  3. We'll come back to you with what we can offer and realistic timing — we're honest about fit; not everything is right for us, and that's fine.

  4. Once agreed, we handle the writing and/or shooting, and let you review before anything goes live.
